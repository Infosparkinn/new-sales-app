Comprehensive Capabilities

Every Single Enterprise Vyapar Feature Implemented

Built ground-up to give Indian merchants, distributors, and retailers complete financial mastery.

Multiple Firms & Companies

Create, switch, and manage separate GSTINs, addresses, signatures, and bank profiles with complete multi-firm isolation.

Generate E-way Bills

Track E-way bill number, transporter ID, vehicle registration, and transportation distance directly embedded on invoices and PDF prints.

Restore Deleted Transactions

Soft-delete safeguard for invoices, expenses, and transactions. Restore any mistakenly deleted record with audit tracking.

Remove Advertisement & Branding

Full control over invoice footer branding. Toggle custom business branding or clean white-label tax invoices with zero ads.

Multiple Pricing Tiers & Party Rates

Set Retail, Wholesale, and Dealer pricing tiers per item. Remember custom negotiated special rates for specific parties.

Update Items in Bulk

Spreadsheet-style bulk price modifier and stock counting tool. Update hundreds of items with percentage increases or fixed shifts.

Party Credit Limits & Warnings

Define maximum outstanding balances per party. Live banner warnings alert you whenever a sale exceeds a customer's credit cap.

Fixed Assets Management

Register business assets (Vehicles, Machinery, IT, Furniture). Automatically computes depreciation and net book values for your Balance Sheet.

Automated WhatsApp Connect

One-click WhatsApp payment reminders with UPI QR codes, instant bill delivery, self-notifications, and service maintenance reminders.

Convert Orders & Challans

Manage Delivery Challans and Quotations. Seamlessly convert pending orders into final GST Sales Invoices in one click.

TCS & TDS Tax Engine

Apply Tax Collected at Source (TCS) and Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) per statutory rules with real-time bill total adjustments.

Live Profit on Invoices

Real-time profit preview shows purchase cost versus selling price margin percentage right as you build customer orders.

Expenses with Input Tax Credit (ITC)

Record operational expenses with vendor GSTIN and CGST/SGST breakdowns. Mark ITC eligibility for seamless GSTR-3B offsets.

Custom Fields, Batch & Serial

Track Manufacturing & Expiry dates, Batch numbers, Serial numbers, and dynamic custom attributes on every inventory item.

Balance Sheet & P&L Reports

Automated double-entry Balance Sheet (Assets = Liabilities + Equity), Billwise Profit & Loss, Partywise Profit & Loss, and Item batch reports.

Cash & Bank Accounts

Track Bank Accounts, Cash in Hand, Cash in GPay / UPI, Credit Cards, and record inter-account fund transfers.

Mobile POS Quick Billing

High-speed touch grid billing for retail store counters with quick Cash, GPay, Card, and Credit buttons with instant thermal print.

Customer Loyalty Points

Reward points program: customers earn points on spending and redeem accumulated points for discounts on checkout.

My Online Store

Instant digital catalog generator. Share active stock items and prices with WhatsApp customers with direct ordering links.

Cross-Device Company Sync

Seamless background multi-device synchronization with connected firms like Sivan sports pro across desktop and mobile.

40+ Reports Hub (7 Categories)

Day Book, GSTR-1, GSTR-2, GSTR-3B, HSN summary, SAC report, Form 27EQ TCS, Cash Flow, Category P&L, and Low stock reports.

10 Business Utilities

Data integrity verification, popup billing calculator, MyBillBook importer, bulk tax slab updater, FY closing, and WhatsApp alerts.

Purchase & Vendor Hub

Purchase Bills, Payment Out, Purchase Return (Debit Notes), and Purchase Orders with automatic inventory stock addition.
